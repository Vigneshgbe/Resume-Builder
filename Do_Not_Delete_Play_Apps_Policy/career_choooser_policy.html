Welcome to Career Chooser ("we," "our," "us," or the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, use, storage, and your rights under applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other international privacy regulations.

1. Information We Collect NO DATA COLLECTED

1.1 No Personal Data Collection

Career Chooser does not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal data.

The app operates entirely on your device without requiring:

  • User registration or account creation
  • Email addresses or contact information
  • Name, age, or demographic information
  • Location data or device identifiers
  • Browsing history or usage analytics
  • Cookies or tracking technologies
  • Payment information
  • Social media credentials

1.2 Local Storage Only

Any interactions you have with the app, such as:

  • Quiz responses and career recommendations
  • Browsing preferences within the app
  • Saved career information

Are stored locally on your device only using HTML5 LocalStorage.

This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any servers
  • Is not accessible to us or any third parties
  • Can be cleared by you at any time by clearing app data

1.3 Automatic Data Collection

We do not use any analytics services, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs that would automatically collect data about your device or usage patterns.

2. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any personal data, we do not use your information for any purpose.

The app functions entirely on your device to provide:

  • Career exploration and guidance
  • Interactive career assessment quizzes
  • Educational content about technology careers
  • Code learning examples

All processing happens locally on your device without any data transmission.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 No Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any information to third parties because we do not collect any data in the first place.

3.2 Third-Party Services

Career Chooser does not integrate with or use any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics platforms (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)

  • Social media integrations

3.3 Legal Requirements

Since we do not collect or possess any user data, we have no information to disclose even if legally required to do so.

4. Data Security

While we do not collect data, we implement security best practices in our app design:

  • Offline Operation: The app works entirely offline, eliminating network-based security risks

17. How to Clear Local Data

If you wish to remove any locally stored information from your device:

For Android Devices:

  1. Go to Settings → Apps
  2. Find and select Career Chooser
  3. Tap Storage
  4. Tap Clear Data or Clear Storage

For iOS Devices:

  1. Go to Settings → General → iPhone Storage
  2. Select Career Chooser
  3. Tap Delete App (reinstalling will start fresh)
