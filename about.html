Built to get out of your way

Resume Builder exists for one reason: writing a resume shouldn't take longer than it needs to.

How it works

Pick a template, type your details into the form, and watch the preview update as you go. When it looks right, download it as a PDF. That's the whole process — there's no account to create and nothing to pay for.

Where your information goes

Everything you type is saved directly in your browser's local storage, on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server while you're building. Closing the tab and coming back later will bring your draft right back, on that same browser.

Why it's free

A resume is one of those things everyone needs at some point, often on short notice. We'd rather make that moment easy than put a paywall in front of it.
